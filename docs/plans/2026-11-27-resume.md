## Thread 1: filters and UI

### Shipped (live)

- `src/lib/filters.svelte.js` is the filter registry. Each filter is defined
  once: URL key, label, group, control kind, options, test and chip label.
  The filter pass, the desktop bar, the phone sheet, counts, Clear all and
  the empty state all read from it. Adding a filter is a one-file change.
- Every filter is in the URL (`region`, `b`, `score`, `swim`, `hi`, `rain`,
  `air`, `safety`, `ns`), so reloads and shared links keep them. Search text
  and "Favorites only" stay out of the URL on purpose: a shared link should
  not show the recipient their own favorites.
- Desktop: Sort, the scoring lens ("Scored for: Balanced"), Cards/Table and
  Compare on one row. Below it: search, Regions, Budget, Score, More filters,
  Favorites. A set button shows its value and its own clear button. Filters
  inside More filters show as removable chips. The count reads
  "N of 121 cities" in a live region.
- Phone: the Refine sheet is rendered from the same registry. "Refine · N"
  counts filters only. View and Compare sit in an uncounted section.
- New filters: air quality, minimum safety, maximum daytime high, maximum
  rain days. Budget steps are round numbers in the visitor's currency.
- Daytime highs moved into the always-loaded data (about 14 KB) so the
  temperature filter does not wait on the detail file.

### Decided, with reasons

- No visa filter. The visa data is hand-typed, unverified and shown nowhere
  on the site. It needs sourcing and a place in the city sheet first.
- No English filter. The city sheet does not show English level, and a
  filter on something the visitor cannot see is confusing.

### Not done

- Not tested with a screen reader or on a real phone (Chrome emulation only).
- The safety filter has two steps and is mild: most cities already pass
  "Very safe (70+)".
- The phone sheet is long (seven sections).
- "The whole thing": only the home page filter area was reworked. The next
  screens, in the order suggested, are the rest of the home page (hero,
  cards, table), then the city sheet, Compare, and My year. The three
  largest files are `MyYear.svelte` (about 3,000 lines), `ThisMonth.svelte`
  and `App.svelte`; take one screen per branch with a preview each.
- `UX_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md`, `MOBILE_DESIGN_PLAN.md` and `TODO.md` predate this
  work and have not been reconciled with it.

## Thread 2: data methodology

### What we learned

1. **The stored cost evidence cannot be re-checked.** `scripts/verify_evidence.py`
   fetched all 606 evidence rows. 86 of 595 quotes (14%) were found on the
   live page or a Wayback copy. 362 pages load but no longer contain the
   quote; 22 links are dead; 44 sites blocked the check. 78 of 127 cities
   have no verifiable quote. The largest sources are the weakest
   (nomads.com 2%, Numbeo 0%). This does not show the prices are wrong:
   aggregator pages change monthly and nothing was saved at collection time.
   Report: `docs/research/2026-11-26-evidence-verification.md`.
2. **Measured rent works where a portal allows it, and most do not.** Trial
   medians against stored rent: Lisbon +6% (Flatio, 20 listings) and −5%
   (casa.sapo, 15), Budapest +46% (20), Berlin +38% (10), Medellín −20%
   (5, four from one operator). Chiang Mai had no usable portal. Flatio
   prices include utilities and the stored rent does not, which explains
   part of the Budapest and Berlin gap. Airbnb, idealista, Uniplaces and
   most local portals forbid automated access or blocked us; we did not
   work around that. Report: `docs/research/2026-11-26-rent-sampling-trial.md`.

### Built

- `scripts/verify_evidence.py` (on `main`): re-checks every evidence quote,
  writes `data/cost-evidence-verification.json`. A full run takes about
  90 minutes, mostly Wayback lookups. Pages are cached outside the repo.
- `scripts/collect_evidence.py` (on the branch): `snapshot` refuses to
  record a number unless the quote is on the page, then stores a dated
  excerpt under `data/evidence-snapshots/` and the full page in
  `~/monsoon-evidence-archive/` (outside git). `--browser` renders
  JavaScript pages; `--wayback` asks the Internet Archive for a copy.
- Check gate (on the branch): `build_costs.py check` fails any evidence row
  dated 2026-11-26 or later without a matching snapshot. Older rows are
  exempt.
- `data/rent-samples/` and `scripts/summarize_rent_samples.py` (on the
  branch): listings, search definition and computed summaries for the
  trial cities.
- Usage notes in `docs/adding-a-city.md`.

### The plan

`docs/research/2026-11-26-data-methodology-plan.md` holds the eight proposals
and the phase order. In short:

- Phase 1: snapshots at collection (done) and measured rent.
- Phase 2: re-collect the other cost components under the new rules for the
  cities with nothing verifiable, storing a low / typical / high range and a
  confidence computed from source count, agreement, age and source tier.
- Later: a fixed published basket, blind double collection, benchmark
  validation, freshness rules on a timer, and sourcing the inputs that are
  still hand-set (property crime, tourist modifier, couple multipliers, rent
  seasonality for 27 cities, 36 components marked only "estimated").

Claims in the plan about outside sources' licences and coverage are marked
"to confirm" and have not been checked.

### Open decisions

1. **Merge `data/snapshots-rent-trial` into `main`.** No user-facing change
   and no deploy. Recommended.
2. **How to define "rent".** The trial recommends the traveler market
   (furnished, 1–3 month stay) with bills included, stated publicly. That
   changes what the rent and utilities lines mean, so `build_costs.py` and
   the city sheet wording would change with it.
3. **How to cover the cities Flatio does not.** Flatio covers about 30
   cities, mostly Portugal, Spain, Czechia, Hungary and Poland, at roughly a
   day of work. The other 90 or so would take 45–70 hours of browsing by
   hand. Before spending that, trial a published dataset: Inside Airbnb is
   the first candidate (licence and city coverage to confirm).
4. **The WHO air-quality licence.** The WHO database is CC BY-NC-SA, which
   matters if the site earns money. Alternatives named in the plan: OpenAQ,
   or CAMS alone.
5. **When measured numbers go live.** Suggested rule: a city's stored rent
   is replaced only when it has at least 20 kept listings; 10–19 is shown as
   a small sample; below 10 the stored figure stays.

### Where to pick up

Start by reading the two reports and the plan, then settle decisions 1–3.
The suggested next build is two pieces in parallel: the Flatio rollout for
the cities it covers, and a published-dataset trial on five cities Flatio
does not cover (Chiang Mai among them). After that, phase 2 on a first
batch of ten cities to learn the cost of re-collection before doing all 127.

Known traps:

- Flatio rendered only 15–19 of 34 cards per page in headless Chrome and has
  no neighbourhood filter, so its numbers are city-wide.
- The "move in within 2 months" rule moves Budapest's median by 12%; keep
  the search definition fixed across cities.
- Prices are asking prices on one date. A second sample in a different
  season is needed before saying anything about seasonality.
- The portal terms in the trial were read by helper agents and were not
  re-read; check them again before a rollout.
- The verifier is strict: translated or paraphrased quotes count as missing,
  and JavaScript-only pages read as empty unless the browser mode is used.
